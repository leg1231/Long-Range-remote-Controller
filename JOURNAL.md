## November 11, 2026: <Ive started mapping the circuit>

<I started reashercing what the PCB would have to do. I need it to insulate the RF traces while still being able to connect a LORA module, ESP 32, and a raspberry pi pico.  FUN FACT: The best way to deal with RF signal leakage from PCB traces to have a 4 layer PCB with 2 ground planes. Im not that rich though.  >
